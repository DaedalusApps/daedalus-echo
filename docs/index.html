What it does

Everything on your device

Six powerful features. Zero cloud dependency.

🎙

Record

Capture audio with your built-in microphone or a paired Bluetooth headset. Stored locally and securely.

📝

Transcribe

Powered by Whisper via sherpa-onnx, every recording becomes searchable text — completely offline.

🧠

AI Summaries

Gemma 3 1B runs entirely on-device to generate titles, summaries, key topics, and mind maps per note.

💬

Ask Your Notes

Ask a natural-language question across your entire library. Semantic search finds the answer, on-device.

🗺

Knowledge Graph

Visualize semantic connections across all your recordings — see which ideas appear together over time.

🔍

Search

Instantly search across all transcripts and summaries. Find any recording by content, not just title.

Requirements

What you need

  • Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher, ARM64
  • 4 GB RAM minimum — 6 GB+ recommended for best performance
  • ~770 MB free storage for AI models, downloaded once on first launch
  • Vulkan 1.1+ recommended for GPU acceleration
  • Tested on Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra · Compatible with most mid-range and flagship Android devices from 2019 onward

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

No data collected · No cloud · No accounts

Short version: Daedalus Echo runs entirely on your device. No personal data is ever collected, transmitted, or stored outside your phone.

1. What data we collect

None. Daedalus Echo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, voice recordings, transcripts, or usage information to any server or third party.

All data you create in the app — recordings, transcripts, summaries, notes — exists exclusively on your device and is never sent anywhere.

2. On-device processing

All AI processing in Daedalus Echo happens locally on your Android device:

  • Speech-to-text — Whisper (via sherpa-onnx) runs entirely on-device
  • Summarization & analysis — Gemma 3 1B runs entirely on-device via MediaPipe
  • Semantic search — Universal Sentence Encoder Lite runs entirely on-device

No audio, text, or query is ever sent to an external server for processing.

3. Network usage

Daedalus Echo requires internet access only once: during the initial setup, the app downloads the AI models (~770 MB total) from public distribution endpoints. This is a one-time download. After setup, the app works fully offline.

The app does not phone home, send analytics, or make any other network requests.

4. Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • RECORD_AUDIO — to record voice notes via the microphone
  • BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT — to connect to Bluetooth microphones
  • INTERNET — for the one-time AI model download described above
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO — to import existing audio files from your device

No permission is used to collect or transmit data.

5. Third-party SDKs and services

Daedalus Echo does not integrate any analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party tracking SDKs. There are no third parties that receive your data.

6. Data storage

All app data (recordings, transcripts, summaries, embeddings) is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all associated data.

7. Children's privacy

Daedalus Echo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
